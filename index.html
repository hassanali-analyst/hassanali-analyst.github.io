About

From economics to analytics —
and now supply chain.

I'm a data analyst who enjoys finding the story hidden inside business data. With a Master's in Economics and over a year of hands-on analytics experience, I work across the full workflow — from cleaning and querying data to building dashboards that tell a clear story.

I currently work as a Procurement / Operations & MIS Analyst in Saudi Arabia, managing purchase orders, invoicing, and operational reporting. Previously I worked as a Junior Data Analyst at Samaya Group and completed a Data Science internship at Hexsoftware. Earlier, three years in banking gave me a sharp eye for accuracy with financial data.

Alongside my data work, I've built focused knowledge in supply chain management and process improvement — completing the Supply Chain Management Specialization (Rutgers University), the Unilever Supply Chain Data Analyst Professional Certificate, the full DMAIC Framework specialization (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control), and the ASQ-Certified Six Sigma Green Belt (CSSGB) Exam Prep, covering logistics, sourcing, inventory management, demand forecasting, SAP fundamentals, root cause analysis, and statistical process control.

I'm now looking for Supply Chain Analyst, Procurement Analyst, Data Analyst, or MIS/Reporting Analyst roles where I can turn data into better decisions.
